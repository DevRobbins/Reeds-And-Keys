Patient teaching, real musicianship.

I'm Emma, an instrumental teacher based in Worcester. I teach clarinet, piano, saxophone and flute to children and adults, in person or online.

I still perform regularly with the Bardi Wind Orchestra, including a Star Wars cantina number at De Montfort Hall, so lessons are shaped by someone who loves playing as much as teaching.

New to music, working through your grades, or returning after years away? You're very welcome.
