01The teacher

Calm, patient, and genuinely encouraging.

I'm Emma, an instrumental teacher based in Worcester. I teach children and adults, from complete beginners to students working through their grades.

Lessons are relaxed and tailored to you, whether that's in person or online. Fancy picking up the dusty instrument from the loft? That's a great place to start too.

Outside of teaching, I play with the Bardi Wind Orchestra, most recently at De Montfort Hall in Leicester.
