Meet your teacher

Hi, I'm Emma

I'm an instrumental teacher based in Worcester, teaching clarinet, piano, saxophone and flute. Whether you're a complete beginner, working towards your next grade, or finally picking up that dusty instrument from the loft, I'll help you enjoy music and keep progressing.

When I'm not teaching, you'll find me playing with the Bardi Wind Orchestra, most recently at De Montfort Hall in Leicester.
